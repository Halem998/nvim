## Decisions

- **D1**: `--allow-scope-collision` bypasses `cross_batch` only, never `in_batch` — an `in_batch`
  collision is a live concurrent co-dispatch hazard with an existing bounded-retry remedy (Tier 1),
  while `cross_batch` (post-task-59, evidence-gated) is exactly the operator-judgment friction an
  override exists to relieve.
- The `~L1841` (now `~L1850`) "eligible again next cycle" instance in `skill-orchestrate/SKILL.md`
  is task-lock acquire-refusal contention, a distinct mechanism from `file_scope_collision` with no
  `in_batch`/`cross_batch` split — its claim is true and was left unchanged.
- `task-lock.md` needed no edit for D1's asymmetry; closed with a `#### Reasoned Exclusions` record.
